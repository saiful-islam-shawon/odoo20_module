Requisition-based multi-vendor sourcing for Odoo 19

Designed for controlled procurement

Create a vendor-free purchase requisition, collect multiple vendor RFQs, compare price, quality and delivery date within the same requisition, then generate multiple purchase orders from the selected vendors.

Requisition-specific comparison

RFQs can only be compared against other RFQs belonging to the same requisition.

Manual sourcing decisions

Recommend, Reject or Cancel each vendor quotation line with no scheduled automation.

Single recommendation per product line

Select one vendor and other offers for that same requisition line are automatically cancelled.

Multiple purchase orders

Recommended lines are grouped vendor-wise and converted into standard Odoo Purchase Orders.
